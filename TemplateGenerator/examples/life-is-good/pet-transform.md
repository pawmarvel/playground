# Life Is Good pet transformation

Transform the supplied user pet into an isolated portrait that can be placed on
the Life Is Good template.

Preserve the pet's recognizable identity, including breed, head shape, muzzle,
eyes, ears, coat structure, and distinctive markings.

Required composition:

- Head and upper chest only.
- Front-facing or nearly front-facing.
- Head upright and centered.
- Friendly, warm expression.
- Complete ears and unclipped portrait silhouette.

Required visual treatment:

- Vintage distressed monochrome screen-print illustration.
- One-color cream or pale warm-yellow ink.
- Strong readable silhouette.
- Simplified grouped fur shapes and selective directional strokes.
- Controlled linework, negative-space shading, and moderate detail.
- Subtle broken ink and speckling.

Do not include a rainbow, paw prints, headline, pet name, footer, garment,
mockup, border, floor, room, scenery, or drop shadow.

Return only the isolated transformed pet asset.
